CourtManager Pro. Executive briefing for professional clubs.

Good morning. I am Ramón del Pozo Rott, founder of CourtManager Pro. In four minutes, this presentation outlines a platform built for elite football and basketball kit operations.

The problem is familiar in every professional dressing room. Equipment is managed with spreadsheets, chat threads and memory. That leads to losses on travel days, sizing mistakes before tip-off or kick-off, duplicated stock and administrative hours that do not improve performance.

CourtManager Pro unifies the full workflow in one web application, usable on mobile in the warehouse, the dressing room or on the road. Roster and sizing, inventory with traceability, material requests, travel packing lists, laundry, medical kit with expiry alerts, pre-match checklist and management reports — one system, with roles and access control.

This is not a consumer app and not a generic ERP. It is designed for first-team reality: multiple competitions, large squads, technical staff and operational excellence.

For innovation programmes such as Real Madrid Next, the project aligns with Performance — because kit and logistics are part of performance — with Cyber Tech — because club data must be protected — and supports E-Health through medical kit control in the dressing room and on trips.

The return is concrete. A club losing fifteen to thirty thousand euros a year in mismanaged equipment recovers a professional plan investment in weeks, not years. Fewer errors. Fewer wasted hours. More control before every match.

We propose a measurable pilot: reduce losses, shorten preparation time and remove ambiguity in sizing and stock — without forcing a cultural shock. The tool follows the real workflow of kit managers and sporting directors.

CourtManager Pro is ready to be presented to multiple elite clubs. Same solution, same standard, adaptable to each club’s colours and operations.
